Executive brief

Every contact center measures the outcome. Almost none measure the cost of getting there.

cci.ai is an additional intelligence layer that unearths the cost hiding inside every customer interaction. It reads interactions your contact center has already handled and surfaces where cost concentrates and where it can be recovered — through shorter handle times, better-targeted agent-assist coaching, digital deflection for contacts that never needed an agent, proactive outreach that prevents the contact altogether, and the system or process failures driving avoidable volume in the first place. Six agents orchestrate the read — extraction, quality grading, cost aggregation, insight synthesis — across every interaction, not a sample.
