Six governed stations between a transcript and a decision.

Ingest & protect

Validate; redact PII before any model sees text.

Read

70+ fields per call — phases, issues, products, sentiment — with self-scored coverage and targeted re-query.

Score

100-pt QA per extraction; low confidence excluded.

Price

KPIs and the three levers in dollars.

Reason

70B model drafts, critiques itself, rewrites — three passes.
