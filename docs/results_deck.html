Discovery is the largest single block (24%) — callers re-explaining what the operator's systems already know. IVR/app context-passing and AI agent-assist attack it directly.

Welcome + auth is 16% pure overhead. Voice biometrics and CTI screen-pop remove most of it without touching the conversation itself.

Agents agree with the data: where they flagged a disproportionate phase, diagnosis led at 36% — per-call flags that point coaching at the right minutes, not at averages.
