What Makes Upsell Work in Telecom Care
Relevance over script
The 89% conversion rate is built on personalized relevance — agents presenting roaming plans to customers calling about travel, or data upgrades to customers hitting limits. Scripted blanket upsell performs at 40–50%.
Timing: post-resolution only
All successful upsells in the corpus occurred after the primary issue was resolved and sentiment was positive. Upselling into a frustrated customer is the primary cause of declined conversions.
What agents are missing (64% of calls)
The 50 calls with no upsell attempt included 19 technical and 14 information calls — many with satisfied end sentiment and clear eligibility signals. An AI copilot surfacing "upsell eligible" at the right moment is the fastest path to 60%+ attempt rate.

Care is already a sales channel — you're just not using it fully. At 89% conversion, the constraint is attempt rate, not close rate. A real-time agent assist flag for eligible calls delivers ROI within 30 days.
